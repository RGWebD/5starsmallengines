FAQ

Good to know

Quick answers before you load up the trailer.

What do you work on?

ATVs and quads, motorcycles and dirt bikes, side-by-sides, jet skis, golf carts, mowers and handheld equipment like trimmers and blowers.

Do you do dyno tuning?

Yes. The shop has its own Dynojet dyno in-house for tuning quads and bikes, including fuel programmer and exhaust setups. Customers drive in from 3 to 4 hours away for it.

What are your hours?

Tuesday to Friday 9 AM to 6 PM and Saturday 9 AM to 2 PM. Closed Sunday and Monday. Holiday hours, such as Thanksgiving and Black Friday, might differ, so call ahead.

Where are you located?

8094 Eisenhower Pkwy, Lizella, GA 31052, just outside Macon.

How much does a repair cost?

It depends on the machine and the problem. Call (478) 808-3415 to describe what is going on and ask about pricing.

Do you sell machines or parts?

The shop sign reads Service · Parts · Sales, and their Facebook bio says Buy + Sell + Trade. Call to ask what is in stock.

What payments do you take?

Credit cards, debit cards and NFC mobile payments.

Is the shop accessible?

Yes. There is a wheelchair-accessible entrance and parking, and a restroom on site.
