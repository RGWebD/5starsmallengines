What we do

Every job, done right

From a carb that won’t idle to a full engine build and a dyno-tuned finish, the whole job happens under one roof on Eisenhower Pkwy.

01 /

Engine rebuilds

Top ends, bottom ends and full overhauls, measured and built right the first time.

02 /

Carburetor work

Rebuilds, cleaning and jetting for bikes, quads and small engines that won’t idle or pull clean.

03 /

Tune-ups & service

Oil, filters, plugs and the preventative maintenance that keeps your machine out of the shop.

04 /

Electrical & bog diagnosis

Intermittent bogs, no-starts and electrical gremlins tracked down instead of parts-swapped.

05 /

In-house dyno tuning

A Dynojet dyno right in the shop. Riders tow in from hours away to get dialed in.

06 /

Programmers & exhaust

Fuel programmer installs, pipes and the dyno time to make the combo actually work.
